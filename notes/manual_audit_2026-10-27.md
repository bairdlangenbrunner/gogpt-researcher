# Repo rules checked against the September 2026 Editing Manual

Written 2026-10-27, before the data center fix in `notes/data_center_backup_plan.md`. Four read-only audits (status and dates; units and capacity; sources, owners and location; discovery and process) compared every rule in `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/sops/`, `docs/reference/`, `docs/country_notes/united_states.md`, the sweep prompt in `.claude/workflows/state-sweep.js`, `scripts/build_state_brief.py`, `scripts/captive_lng.py`, `scripts/schema_constants.py` and `scripts/worklist.py` with the manual text. Every line cited below was re-read by hand. Manual line numbers refer to the plain-text export of the September 2026 manual; "M396" means line 396 of that export.

The three distillations (`lifecycle_rules.md`, `unit_conventions.md`, `controlled_vocab.md`) still say they were made from the March 2026 manual. Most of what follows is residue from that version plus a handful of repo rulings that go past the manual.

## A. Rules that say the opposite of the manual

1. **Data center backup engine sets.** `scripts/captive_lng.py` line 601 ("Emergency or backup generator sets at a data center are not captive power plants and are not watch items; only generation that supplies routine power counts"), `docs/country_notes/united_states.md` line 49, `docs/sops/update.md` step 9 and `docs/workflows.md` section 2. The manual's Emergency/Backup checkbox (M396) only appears when Captive is ticked and the industry type is Data Centre, so a backup unit is a captive unit in the database. M578 adds engine sets at 50 MW total. Nothing in the manual excludes backup sets. Not a ruling by anyone; a misreading from 2026-10-09. Fix is the plan.

2. **Gas-fired data center plants held on the watch list** (`data_center_hold`, Baird 2026-10-08; `captive_lng.py` line 592, `schema_constants.py` `WATCH_CASES`, `CLAUDE.md`, `discovery.md`, `update.md`). The manual adds any publicly reported project (M214, M661) and names data center plants "XYZ Data Center power station" (M237). It has no hold. Baird's call.

3. **Notes can never be cleaned up.** `CLAUDE.md` line 98 and `update.md` line 224: "never a rewrite, never a shortening, never a deletion" (Baird 2026-10-07). The manual at M272: "Also feel free to clean up old/outdated notes if they are clearly no longer needed/applicable." Status Detail has no such sentence in the manual (M689), so the additive rule for Status Detail is only stricter, not contrary. Baird's call; the gate `additive` enforces both boxes the same way.

4. **The Planned checkbox.** `lifecycle_rules.md` lines 76 to 79 and 98 to 101, `update.md` lines 94 and 182 still say to record a projected year and tick Planned. The manual (M693, M694, M709) records a planned start or retirement as a scheduled event with an Event Year and a Source Year. The repo's own later section (`lifecycle_rules.md` lines 122 to 145) already says this. Stale March text. Safe fix.

5. **The December rule.** `lifecycle_rules.md` lines 149 to 152 ("roll the start/retirement year forward"), `update.md` line 95 ("push unconfirmed current-year dates to the following year"), `build_state_brief.py` around line 430 ("move the planned year forward") and `review_app/checklist.py` row 23. The manual (M192): add a new scheduled event with the following year as Event Year and the current year as Source Year, and do not edit the existing entry. Safe fix.

6. **Engine count fields.** `state-sweep.js` lines 388 to 389: "Number Of Engines and Capacity Per Engine are for reciprocating engines (internal combustion) only. Never fill them for turbines." The manual (M608) uses those fields for aeroderivative gas turbine sets too; only frame turbines take a direct capacity (M601). Safe fix.

7. **Captive fields on a plant that exports.** `state-sweep.js` lines 390 to 392: captive fields "stay blank for a plant that sells to the grid". The manual (M380, M390) says captive plants may sell a large share of their power outside, and Captive Non-Industry Use records that. Safe fix: reword to "a plant whose main customer is the grid".

8. **Combined cycle built from older GT and ST units.** `unit_conventions.md` lines 61 to 64: "replace the separate GT1/ST1 unit entries with one new CC1 unit". The manual (M702 to M704): add a retired milestone to GT1 and ST1, do not delete them, and set "Use of GT in CC set" and "Use of ST in CC set" with the new unit's ID. Safe fix.

9. **Town-center coordinates.** `location.md` line 106 never fills a coordinate with a town center for a plant whose site is unknown. The manual (M347) says for proposed plants "you often just have a city. Do your best to choose an appropriate location", and M361 says to put such a point in an open area. Part of the location ladder built 2026-10-20. Baird's call; a small one.

## B. Rules stricter than the manual (no conflict, but the manual does not require them)

- Two independent publishers for a green status change; one validated source for everything else; the color tiers (Baird 2026-10-02 and 2026-10-08). The manual says only "Add a datasource for every data entry" (M219).
- Every URL through `url_verifier.py`; gem.wiki, globalenergymonitor.org and GEM-derived republishers never cited; abarrelfull banned; lead sites (Cleanview, interconnection.fyi, gridstatus.io, GridInfo, Wikipedia) stay yellow (Baird 2026-10-23). All repo rulings.
- Owner changes need a dated, closed transaction from two publishers (`state-sweep.js` line 376). The manual (M322) only notes a pending acquisition.
- Exact location needs the footprint or named parcel seen in imagery (`location.md` line 41, `state-sweep.js` line 435, gate `location`). The manual (M355 to M359) accepts five routes to exact that need no imagery: coordinates on a government, owner or operator site; coordinates in a permit application; a map embedded on the owner's site; Wikimapia or OpenStreetMap; a project Google Maps identifies by name. The repo also never reopens an exact plant (Baird 2026-10-20) and skips City on exact plants (Baird 2026-10-21), where M333 asks to fill in as much as possible. Worth a decision: the agent may be downgrading permit coordinates to approximate.
- Plant-level validation errors are always fixes (Baird 2026-10-21). The manual (M198) makes project-level errors on co-located coal and gas plants optional.
- Captive LNG: only the LNG team's Qualifying rows are at the add threshold (Baird 2026-10-08). The manual's Captive section (M379 to M400) has no such list.
- Latest Activity only for in-development, shelved and inferred units, and only when the newest report is a year old or older (Baird 2026-10-05). The manual (M691) sets no age and no status limit beyond in-development.
- Rounding to the whole MW is mandatory in `controlled_vocab.md` line 84 but the sweep prompt (line 343) asks for one decimal. The manual says "can round" (M610). Pick one.

## C. Rules looser than the manual, and gaps

- **Inferred statuses carry no datasource.** `state-sweep.js` lines 328 to 340 and `CLAUDE.md` ("inferred statuses excepted"). The manual (M621) requires a datasource on every timeline entry, and M637 sets the milestone year as the last-activity date plus two or four years. `lifecycle_rules.md` lines 142 to 145 still say to confirm the year rule before the first inferred batch; the September manual answers it. Fix: the inferred entry cites the Latest Activity source and carries the computed year.
- **Worklist ladder.** `scripts/worklist.py` applies the cancelled-inferred test to shelved units (the manual lists only announced, pre-construction and construction at M675 and M679), orders mothballed units fourth where the manual's fourth tier is "units with planned retirement" (M213), and counts calendar years rather than dates. `CLAUDE.md` router repeats the mothballed tier.
- **Capacity wording.** `CLAUDE.md` line 101 and `unit_conventions.md` lines 153 to 160 say "GENERATING MW only". The manual wants nameplate or installed capacity (M603) and defines "generating capacity" as the lower, operational figure (M606). The intent (electrical nameplate, never mechanical drive, never MWt) is right; the word is wrong. Reword to "electrical nameplate MWe".
- **EU and UK threshold.** The manual's text (M593) reads "<20MW per unit", almost certainly a typo for 20 MW or more; it does not say whether 20 MW applies per engine or to the summed set. The repo sums. Ask Dan.
- **US-only steps.** The data center search (row 39) and the IRP step run only for US scopes; the manual's data center naming (M237), Project Links (M378), the Emergency/Backup checkbox (M396) and the IRP rules (M588 to M591) are not US-limited.
- **Not written anywhere in the repo:** the Project Links field (M378); the "XYZ Data Center power station" and "XYZ LNG terminal power station" naming (M237); the current-name rule for manufacturers, GE Vernova for in-development units and the document's name for older ones (M555, M556), and the model-not-package tip (M562); "Use of GT in a CC set" and the "change of plans" type with the old unit's ID (M436, M729 to M748); the replacement start year never before the old unit's retirement (M435); a maintenance change that moves the retirement date becomes a new scheduled retirement event (M427); the "added" research status for new units (M478) and the close-out filter for leftover "in progress" units (M191); no scheduled operating event for shelved or cancelled units, with earlier ones kept as history (M695); every scheduled event needs an Event Year (M653); a wrong timeline entry goes to the PM first (M623); equal-share owners (M330) and "unknown" ownership (M299); Name (Other), Language and Local Name (M263 to M270); no "county", "township" or "district" words in Local Area and Major Area, one nearest city, and city-states repeated in City and Subnational (M365 to M368); the Asana step for a new Other IDs system (M375, M717); EU Other IDs (M376); the "Additional notes" column in the assignments sheet (M199).
- `docs/sops/qc.md` line 26 says a QC failure is fixed inside the running batch; line 73 and `CLAUDE.md` say fixes route to a follow-on Update batch.

## Resolutions (Baird, 2026-10-27, later the same day)

Baird's instruction: change the researcher rules to match the manual, with one exception. The researcher never cleans up, shortens or rewrites Notes; that stays a human job. Each item below says what the repo now does.

**Kept as repo policy (the manual does not require them and they do not contradict it).** These are how the agent proves a value for human review, not what gets recorded: every URL through `url_verifier.py`; the color tiers, including two independent publishers for a green status change (one publisher is still a staged change, colored yellow); gem.wiki, globalenergymonitor.org and GEM-derived republishers never cited; abarrelfull banned; Wayback copies citable; lead sites yellow; a confirmed status change is never punted to a qa note; the entity lane never creates entities. Status Detail stays additive (the manual has no cleanup sentence for it). Notes stays additive for the researcher by Baird's choice; an outdated note is flagged in a qa record with concern type `notes_cleanup` for a human to remove, and the gate `additive` keeps enforcing both boxes.

**Changed to match the manual.**

1. Backup engine fleets at data centers are plants at the summed nameplate threshold (50 MW, 20 MW in the EU and UK), with Backup Power yes at unit level, Captive industry type data center, Captive industry use power, internal combustion, Number Of Engines and Capacity Per Engine from the permit, named "<Operator> <Place> Data Center power station", the data center named in Project Links. Capacity unknown means add with capacity blank (M597), not a watch item. The step runs in every scope, not only US states.
2. The `data_center_hold` watch case is gone. A gas-fired data center plant is added like any other project; its status comes from the manual's ladder.
3. A projected start or retirement is a scheduled event: Event Year is the projected year, Source Year is the year the source reported it. The staged `Start year` or `Planned retire` value carries the Event Year and the action tells the human to add a new scheduled event entry, never to change an existing one. No Planned checkbox anywhere.
4. December rule: an in-development or retiring unit with a scheduled event in the ending year and no source confirming it gets a new scheduled event with the following year as Event Year and the current year as Source Year. The existing entry is never edited.
5. Number Of Engines and Capacity Per Engine are filled for internal combustion sets and aeroderivative gas turbine sets; a frame gas turbine is one turbine per unit with its capacity in Capacity (MW).
6. Captive fields are filled for every captive plant, including one that exports; Captive non-industry use records the export (power, heat, both, none).
7. A combined cycle made from older GT and ST units: add CC1 with an operating milestone, add retired milestones to GT1 and ST1 for the same year, never delete them, set "Use of GT in CC set" and "Use of ST in CC set" on the old units with the CC unit's ID.
8. A proposed plant with only a city gets an approximate point placed in an open area near that city, Location Data Source the Google Maps link plus the source that named the city.
9. Exact location follows the manual's five routes, none of which needs imagery: coordinates published by a government body, operator or owner; coordinates in a permit application or similar document; a map embedded on the owner's or operator's site; Wikimapia or OpenStreetMap; a project Google Maps identifies by name. The satellite look and the georeferenced map stay as extra routes. The 500 m move check stays as a review flag, never a block. City, Local area, Major area and Subnational are filled when blank on any plant in scope, exact plants included; coordinates on an exact plant are still not reopened.
10. Validation: a project-level error on a plant that also has coal units, and a unit-level error on a coal-to-gas conversion, is optional (M198, M220). The gate `validation` accepts "optional under the manual" as the answer for those items and still requires every other item answered.
11. The captive LNG workbook is a lead list. Any captive LNG plant verified at the threshold is an add; the workbook's Qualifying rows are the ones to check first. Compressor-drive turbines are still excluded (not electrical nameplate).
12. Latest Activity follows M691: in-development units that are not progressing get the most recent source and the date it last mentioned the unit. The repo's working reading of "for some time" stays one year; shelved and inferred units keep it because the inferred statuses are computed from it.
13. Rounding is optional (M610): record the figure the source gives, at most one decimal; rounding to the whole MW is allowed, never required.
14. An inferred status is a milestone entry with a datasource: the Latest Activity source, with the milestone year equal to the Latest Activity date plus two years (shelved) or four years (cancelled), per M637. It is no longer the exception to the orphan-value rule.
15. The worklist ladder is the manual's: in-development; shelved and shelved-inferred; planned retirement in the current year; planned retirement in any year; then the rest. The cancelled-inferred test applies only to announced, pre-construction and construction units. Arithmetic runs on the Latest Activity date, not the calendar year.
16. Capacity is nameplate or installed electrical capacity in MW (M603), never the lower operational figure, never mechanical drive, never MWt.
17. Written into the reference docs and the sweep prompt for the first time: Project Links; "XYZ Data Center power station" and "XYZ LNG terminal power station" naming; the current manufacturer name for in-development units (GE Vernova) and the document's name for older units; model not package; "Use of GT in a CC set", "Use of ST in a CC set", "change of plans" and "replacement" with the linked unit ID; replacement start year never before the old unit's retirement; a maintenance change to the retirement date is a new scheduled retirement event, never a new operating milestone; the "added" research status on a new unit and the close-out filter of leftover "in progress" units; no scheduled operating event for a shelved or cancelled unit, earlier ones kept; every scheduled event has an Event Year; a wrong timeline entry goes to the project manager through a qa record; equal-share owners, "unknown" owner, blank owner on a vague proposal, pending acquisition goes to Notes and the possible-updates sheet; Name (Other), Language and Local Name; Local area and Major area without "county", "township" or "district", one nearest city, city-states repeated in City and Subnational; a new Other IDs system goes to the project manager through a qa record; EU Other IDs; the "Additional notes" column at close-out; "Disrupted due to conflict" is the checkbox's label.
18. `docs/sops/qc.md`: a QC finding is fixed in a follow-on Update batch, matching line 73 and the router.

## What this means for the data center plan

The manual supports every part of the plan: backup units live under Captive plus Data Centre (M396), engine sets are added at 50 MW summed (M578, M593), capacity may be blank until found (M597), the plant is named "XYZ Data Center power station" (M237), and the data center goes in Project Links (M378). The two rules the plan removes (backup sets excluded; gas-fired data centers held) are the ones with no manual basis. The open questions for Amalia and Dan in the plan note stand, plus the EU and UK per-engine question above.
