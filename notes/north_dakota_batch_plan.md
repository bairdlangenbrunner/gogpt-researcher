## State context the sweep needs

- Grid operators: the Southwest Power Pool covers most of the state (Basin Electric, Montana-Dakota Utilities, Western Area Power Administration). The Midcontinent Independent System Operator covers the eastern edge (Otter Tail Power, Minnkota, Great River Energy, Northern States Power around Fargo and Grand Forks). Both queues are mandatory.
- Regulators: the North Dakota Public Service Commission sites generating plants of 50 MW or more under Chapter 49-22 (a letter of intent, then a certificate of site compatibility; a jurisdictional determination case decides whether a plant is exempt, as for Applied Digital's backup generation in PU-26-155). The Department of Environmental Quality Division of Air Quality issues permits to construct and Title V permits; public notices are posted per facility.
- Utilities to read: Basin Electric Power Cooperative (Bison, Pioneer, Lonesome Creek, River Run with NextEra), Great River Energy (Spiritwood), Minnkota Power Cooperative (Harmony Township plant, 400 MW), Montana-Dakota Utilities (Heskett), Otter Tail Power, Northern States Power (Xcel), Rainbow Energy Center (Coal Creek owner), Applied Digital, Nakota Energy, Base Electron (with Babcock and Wilcox).
- The state has Bakken flare-gas captive generation (bitcoin miners and field power) and several data center campuses with on-site generation. The data center step is mandatory.
