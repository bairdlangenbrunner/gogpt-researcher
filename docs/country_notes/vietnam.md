# Vietnam

Gas-heavy fleet, with the national Power Development Plan (PDP) driving what
gets built and when — the PDP number-in-roman-numerals is the reference point
for whatever plans are current.

## Regulators & official sources

- **Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT)** — publishes the Power Development
  Plan (PDP). As of the source doc, the relevant plan was the draft **PDP
  VIII** ("Vietnam's Energy Development Strategy to 2030 and vision to 2045"
  / "National Electricity Development Plan VIII" for 2021-2030 with a vision
  to 2045). At the time it was expected mid-2021 but was anticipated to slip;
  as of 2026 there have almost certainly been newer PDP revisions or a
  successor plan — check MOIT's site for the current version rather than
  citing the VIII-era draft as current.
  - Draft PDP 8 source page:
    [moit.gov.vn draft report](https://moit.gov.vn/tin-tuc/thi-truong-nuoc-ngoai/du-thao-bao-cao-quy-hoach-tong-the-ve-nang-luong-quoc-gia-th.html) —
    also references a "Draft Report of the National Energy Master Plan for
    2021-2030, with a vision to 2050," which PDP work is meant to harmonize
    with.

## Key operators & utilities

- **EVN (Vietnam Electricity)** — the main electricity generating company;
  publishes an annual report in English. As of the source doc, the most
  recent found was the [2018 EVN Annual Report](https://www.evn.com.vn/userfile/User/tcdl/files/2019/8/EVNAnnualReport2018(1).pdf)
  (released ~Aug 2019) — proposed/under-construction plants on p.32,
  existing plants on pp.34-35. Check EVN's site for a current edition before
  relying on the 2018 figures.
- **Genco 1, Genco 2, Genco 3** — three generation companies alongside EVN;
  their own websites may carry more current proposed/under-construction data
  than the EVN annual report.

## Preferred sources (beyond the global roster)

- [Mekong Infrastructure Tracker (Stimson Center)](https://www.stimson.org/2020/mekong-infrastructure-tracker-tool/#) —
  potentially useful for proposed/under-construction plants.
- Vietnam Energy news, in [English](http://nangluongvietnam.vn/news/en/home)
  and [Vietnamese](http://nangluongvietnam.vn/news/vn/trang-chu) — has an
  electricity section; not all articles are published in both languages, so
  check the Vietnamese edition too if searching only in English comes up
  short.
- **Vietnam Initiative for Energy Transition (VIETSE)** — [vietse.vn](https://vietse.vn/) —
  NGO publishing energy-policy analysis (e.g. a 2020 report on climate-policy
  developments).
- [Vietnamese Wikipedia power plants page](https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danh_s%C3%A1ch_c%C3%A1c_nh%C3%A0_m%C3%A1y_%C4%91i%E1%BB%87n_t%E1%BA%A1i_Vi%E1%BB%87t_Nam) —
  as of the source doc, appeared well-referenced and reasonably current, and
  draws on GEM's own Global Coal Plant Tracker data plus official sources for
  coal; possibly maintained in connection with VIETSE, which links to it.
  Treat as a lead list to confirm against primary sources.

## Research tips

- IEEFA has (or had) a Vietnam-focused analyst; if you need an outside expert
  read on the sector, IEEFA's Vietnam energy research is worth checking for a
  current point of contact via their website rather than an old contact
  saved from a prior batch.
- Because not all Vietnam Energy articles are bilingual, searching in
  Vietnamese as well as English may surface different results.
- A team note flagged a possible Vietnam gas-plant source surfaced via a
  Chinese-language social media account; treat any such source as an
  unverified lead only, not citable on its own.

## Gotchas

- The PDP VIII referenced in the source material was still in draft/expected
  status as of 2021; treat any "PDP VIII" citation as potentially superseded
  by a later PDP revision — confirm which PDP is officially in force before
  citing plan-level capacity targets.
- EVN's annual report was last found in a 2018 edition (released 2019) as of
  the source doc — likely several editions stale by now.
