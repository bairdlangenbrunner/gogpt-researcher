# Nigeria

Large, gas-heavy fleet — gas is the dominant source of thermal generation, so
most generation-by-company data doubles as a lead list for gas plants worth
looking up individually.

## Regulators & official sources

- **Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC)** — the core regulator.
  - [NESI generation statistics by plant](https://www.nerc.gov.ng/index.php/home/nesi/403-generation) —
    includes GPS coordinates, gas supply source, number of units, and start
    year for ~23 plants. Undated file; as of 2021 it appeared to lag behind
    company-reported data by a few years — re-verify currency before citing.
  - "NESI Key Operational & Financial Data for 2019-2020" (uploaded Mar 2021) —
    average available generation capacity and plant-level ownership for 2020.
  - [Licensee page](https://nerc.gov.ng/index.php/licencees) — companies holding
    generation licenses and their licensed capacities.
  - [Generation by each genco](https://nerc.gov.ng/index.php/library/industry-statistics/generation/549-energy-sent-out-to-grid-by-each-genco#data) —
    as of the 2019-era doc, data shown only went up to 2018; check whether this
    has been kept current.
- **Energy Commission of Nigeria** — maintains (or maintained) a National
  Energy Databank; as of the source doc it was offline for "maintenance" with
  no Wayback Machine snapshot. Worth checking if it's back.
- **Nigerian Federal Ministry of Power** — [press releases](http://www.power.gov.ng/news-media/press-releases/)
  on government-owned plants coming online.
- **Transmission Company of Nigeria (TCN)** — published a "Transmission
  Expansion Plan / Power System Master Plan" (Dec 2017); useful for
  system-level context even if dated.

## Key operators & utilities

- **Niger Delta Power Holding Company (NDPHC)** — [generation page](http://ndphc.net/generation)
  with plant-level detail; company behind the National Integrated Power
  Project (NIPP, initiated 2004).
- **Egbin Power** — operator of the large Egbin plant; company site at
  egbin-power.com has been used to cross-check NERC data currency.
- Individual gencos identified via the NERC "generation by each genco" list
  are worth checking directly for owner-published plant data, since gas is
  such a large share of Nigerian generation.

## Preferred sources (beyond the global roster)

- [Power Africa (AfDB)](https://powerafrica.opendataforafrica.org/) — key
  source for identifying gas plants including under-construction/proposed;
  has Google Maps-based location data.
- [African Energy](https://www.africa-energy.com/database) — potentially more
  current than Power Africa, includes primary fuel and location; some data
  requires subscription.
- [List of power stations in Nigeria (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_power_stations_in_Nigeria) —
  reasonable starting point; treat as a lead list to confirm against primary
  sources, not a citable source itself.
- [Nigerian National Integrated Power Project (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_National_Integrated_Power_Project) —
  background on the NIPP program; see also the Oct 2020 National Integrated
  Infrastructure Master Plan.
- ESI Africa news site — searchable for commissioning/construction-start
  announcements (e.g. Egbin 2019 commissioning article, Kingline Power
  Project 2019 article).
- "The Nigerian Energy Sector" (EU / Nigerian Energy Support Programme / GIZ,
  2015) — company-level capacity listing as of that year; useful as a
  historical baseline, not current data.
- JICA "Current State of Primary Energy in Nigeria" report and its annex
  (appears to date to ~2018) — unit-level list including UC and proposed
  plants.
- "Update of the ECOWAS revised master plan for the development of power
  generation and transmission of electrical energy" — has a "short-term
  investments up to 2022" section for Nigeria.

## Research tips

- Historically (pre-GOGPT), WRI's Nigeria gas-plant work relied heavily on a
  now-defunct site, "Nidel Power" (2013 vintage) — not available as a live
  source any more, flagging only so it isn't chased.
- Cross-check NERC's plant list against company-published data (e.g. Egbin
  Power's own site) to gauge how current NERC's numbers actually are — the
  doc that seeded this note found NERC's file undated but seemingly a few
  years stale.
- ESI Africa is a reasonable place to search for plant-specific commissioning
  and construction news when official sources go quiet.

## Gotchas

- Ownership data in NERC sources is flagged by NERC itself as potentially not
  up to date — don't treat it as current without a corroborating source.
- The Energy Commission of Nigeria's National Energy Databank was offline as
  of the source doc's writing with no archived version found; if you find it
  live now, note the date you confirmed that.
