# India

Large thermal fleet with genuinely good official data — the Central
Electricity Authority (CEA) is a strong primary source, unusually so for a
country this size, but its various publications cover different slices
(annual lists vs. monthly fuel/status reports) and need to be cross-referenced.

## Regulators & official sources

- **Central Electricity Authority (CEA)** — comprehensive, timely data on
  power stations.
  - Annual **List of Thermal Power Stations** (via cea.nic.in).
  - CEA's Fuel Management Division publishes a monthly "Monthly Report on
    Fuel Supply for Gas Based Power Stations" (fuel type and quantities
    consumed per plant) — historically at `cea.nic.in/monthlycoal.html`; the
    doc that seeded this note is from the pre-2022 CEA site structure, so
    confirm the current URL on the CEA website rather than assuming the old
    link resolves.
- **Ministry of Power** — issues a monthly "**Report on Broad Status of
  Thermal Power Projects**" (historically at `cea.nic.in/monthlybs.html`,
  same caveat on URL currency) — covers plants under construction and
  planned additions.
  - [Power sector at a glance – All India](https://powermin.gov.in/en/content/power-sector-glance-all-india)
  - [Ministry overview page](https://powermin.gov.in/en/content/overview)
  - [NPP dashboard](https://npp.gov.in/dashBoard/cp-map-dashboard) — mapped
    plant-level dashboard.

## Key operators & utilities

- **NTPC** — India's largest power generation company; individual plant
  detail (e.g. Lara Thermal Power Project, Mauda TPP) has historically been
  documented in third-party trade databases as well as NTPC's own materials.

## Preferred sources (beyond the global roster)

- **Infraline Energy** ("India's premier research, consultancy and solution
  provider") — has detailed pages on individual power plants; useful when CEA
  data needs a plant-level cross-check. Note this is a commercial research
  provider, not a primary/government source — treat as corroborating, not
  authoritative.

## Research tips

- The CEA "annual list" and "monthly fuel/status reports" are complementary,
  not redundant: use the annual thermal list to establish which plants exist,
  and the monthly gas-fuel-supply and Broad Status reports to track
  under-construction status and fuel-consumption currency.
- CEA and Ministry of Power URLs in older source material point at
  `cea.nic.in` paths that may have moved; search the current CEA and
  powermin.gov.in sites for the equivalent current report rather than
  assuming an old link still works.

## Gotchas

- None specifically flagged in the source material beyond general URL/link
  rot on the CEA site — but given how central CEA is to this country's
  research, confirm you're looking at the current-year Broad Status report
  and not an archived/stale one before citing capacity or status figures.
