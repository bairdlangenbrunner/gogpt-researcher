- Last Updated: 2026-11-12
- Researcher: Amalia Llano

```
NF 6/17/25: PSCW Docket ID: 6630-CE-317 The maps shows two locations next to each other: proposed and alternative https://web.archive.org/web/20250302133838/https://psc.wi.gov/Documents/HighlightedCases/OakCreekGenerationMap.pdf

Gregor, Nov 2024: This brand new five-unit, 1100 MW gas plant is to be located "directly west of the existing Oak Creek Power Plant (OCPP) and Elm Road Generating Station (ERGS) on land owned by Wisconsin Electric in the City of Oak Creek, Wisconsin." 
See p19 (labeled as p 1-5) of this doc: https://apps.psc.wi.gov/ERF/ERFview/viewdoc.aspx?docid=505447

Note that GEM refers to the pre-existing neighboring plants as South Oak Creek Plant (Project ID: L100000104277) and Elm Road power station (Project ID: L100000104274 ), respectively.

"The Project...a natural gas electric generating facility (the Project) with a capacity of approximately 1,100 megawatts (MW)... will consist of five new GE 7FA.05 combustion turbine generators (CTGs)."  "The Facility includes five (5) natural gas fired advanced F-Class units with an approximate output of 220 MW each."
See pp19-20 (labeled as pp 1-5 and 1-6) of this doc: https://apps.psc.wi.gov/ERF/ERFview/viewdoc.aspx?docid=505447

The new plant's total capacity of 1100 MW is confirmed on p 21 of 53 (labeled as p18) of this April 2024 WEC Energy Group Investor Update:
https://d18rn0p25nwr6d.cloudfront.net/CIK-0000783325/5947be6c-a832-4027-bf46-0ee090b07776.pdf

We previously listed these gas-fired expansion units in various configurations under Project IDs L100000104277 and L100000104274, but after consultation with Jenny, I have deleted those units at the old locations and reassigned them to this new project ID L100001025603 to make it clear that they are a distinct expansion project and not a coal-to-gas conversion of existing units.

While these five units are being built from scratch, they will make use of some pre-existing infrastructure associated with the South Oak Creek coal-fired power station: "The CT units would be interconnected to the existing onsite interconnection point currently utilized by Oak Creek Units 5 and 6" (GEM refers to these as *South* Oak Creek units 5 and 6, which were retired in 2024): https://psc.wi.gov/Pages/CommissionActions/CasePages/OakCreekGeneration.aspx

The fact that these five brand-new gas-fired units are entirely separate from the coal-to-gas conversion units scheduled for Units 1 and 2 of the adjacent Elm Road power station is made clear on  p2 of the following doc: https://apps.psc.wi.gov/ERF/ERFview/viewdoc.aspx?docid=490382:
"...simple-cycle natural gas combustion turbine units totaling approximately 1,100 to 1,200 MW at the Oak Creek Power Plant campus (“OCPP CT”) with anticipated commercial operation dates for the units occurring from fall 2027 through June 2028. These are in addition to the future planned addition of 100% gas capability at the Elm Road Generating Station (ERGS)..."

Early announcements about this gas-fired expansion called for only 300 MW of new gas-fired power: https://www.jsonline.com/story/money/business/energy/2020/11/06/we-energies-plans-shut-down-oak-creek-power-plant-2024/6191575002/
However, the more recent official documents cited above make it clear that the project has expanded to include five units totaling 1100 MW.
```

## State context

- **Grid.** The whole state is in MISO. American Transmission Company owns the eastern and central grid. Xcel's Northern States Power-Wisconsin and Dairyland Power Cooperative are in the west. MISO's queue is an open JSON file. Newer requests often have the state blank, so they are matched by transmission owner.
- **Regulator.** The Public Service Commission of Wisconsin (PSC) approves any plant of 100 MW or more, utility or merchant, with a Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity (case type CE). Smaller utility plants come under a Certificate of Authority (case type CA). Docket numbers start with the utility's number:
  - 6630: Wisconsin Electric (We Energies).
  - 6690: Wisconsin Public Service.
  - 6680: Wisconsin Power and Light (Alliant).
  - 3270: Madison Gas and Electric.
  - 5: joint dockets.
  - 9835 and 9837: Invenergy's project companies.

  Its filing system is ERF (`apps.psc.wi.gov/ERF`), open without a sign-in.
- **No integrated resource plan rule.** Wisconsin utilities do not file resource plans. What plays that role:
  - the PSC's Strategic Energy Assessment (the 2026 draft came out in June 2026 and counts 5.4 GW of planned new or upgraded gas);
  - the utilities' investor updates and capital plans;
  - the CE dockets themselves;
  - Dairyland's and WPPI's own plans;
  - Xcel's Upper Midwest plan, filed in Minnesota.

  All of them are listed in `scopes/us-wi/irp_plans.json` and read in full each batch.
- **Air permits.** The Department of Natural Resources (DNR) permits the whole state, Milwaukee included. Its complete record is WARP permit tracking (`apps.dnr.wi.gov/warp_ext/`), searched by facility name or industry code. Each facility has a facility ID (FID), and every permit document downloads from it. Notices of draft permits are on the DNR public notice page. Tribal land goes to EPA Region 5.
- **Utilities and owners to read.**
  - We Energies and WPS (WEC Energy Group): South Oak Creek turbines, Paris RICE, Fox North, Elm Road, Weston, Valley, Port Washington, Concord, Germantown, Paris, Pulliam, De Pere, Fox, West Marinette and Weston RICE. We Energies is also applying to buy Red Oak Ridge and Foundry Ridge.
  - Alliant (Wisconsin Power and Light): Edgewater 5, Columbia, Neenah, Sheboygan Falls, Riverside and Rock River.
  - Madison Gas and Electric: Blount Street, West Campus, the diesel generators, Fitchburg (which GEM lacks), and its shares of Elm Road, Columbia, West Marinette 34 and RockGen.
  - Xcel (Northern States Power-Wisconsin): French Island and Wheaton.
  - Dairyland: Elk Mound (which GEM lacks), Madgett, Genoa and Nemadji Trail.
  - WPPI Energy: South Fond du Lac and Island Street.
  - Invenergy: Forest Junction, Union Depot, Red Oak Ridge and Foundry Ridge.
  - Merchant owners: RockGen, LSP-Whitewater and Port Washington Generating Station LLC.
  - Municipal: Marshfield.
- **Data centers.**
  - Already in GEM: Microsoft Mount Pleasant (diesel backup fleets in three units) and Vantage Lighthouse WI-1 in Port Washington (45 diesel engines, about 87 MW).
  - Not in GEM: Microsoft Kenosha and Caledonia, later Vantage phases, Meta Beaver Dam (Alliant service agreement, docket 6680-TE-115) and any other campus `data_center_fleets.py` lists.
  - Each is answered under `data_centers`.
