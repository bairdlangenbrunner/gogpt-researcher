## Findings that argue for no missing plants
1. Cleanview (13 planned plants, 6,809 MW) and the EIA-860M August 2026 planned sheet were both read in full. Illinois gas entries in EIA-860M planned: Lincoln Land (2 x 638.4 MW), ITT Cogen (2 x 4.1 MW), and a 2.5 MW oil unit at Bushnell (McDonough County, under construction, 2027, below any threshold). The Canceled or Postponed sheet was read on 2026-10-30 from the September 2026 file (work/eia/september_generator2026.xlsx). Its only Illinois gas rows are Zion Energy Center CTG4 and CTG5, 198.9 MW each, proposals that the plant's EIA status history dates to 2008 and shows withdrawn by 2014; EIA gives no cancellation date, the units fall before GEM's 2020 start for cancelled units, and they get no record. The other Illinois rows are coal (Prairie Energy; Taylorville, which GEM already lists as cancelled), wood, wind, solar and batteries.
2. Vistra's 10-Q for the quarter ended March 2026 lists Baldwin, Kincaid and Newton as coal retirements by 2027; it names gas repowering only for Coleto Creek (Texas) and Miami Fort (Ohio) and says others are "being evaluated". Illinois closure notices: Newton Oct 2027, Kincaid Jan 2028, Baldwin Feb 2028 (via search summaries, snippet). No Illinois gas conversion announced.
3. Constellation 10-Q (June 2026): Zion Energy Center plans to run past January 2030. No Illinois additions mentioned (snippet).
4. Springfield CWLP letter to the Illinois EPA and Illinois Power Agency dated 2026-02-13 (read in full via fetch.py): fleet is Dallman 4 (200 MW coal) plus 144 MW of peakers; no new gas planned. A stakeholder filing in the Illinois Power Agency process argues new gas over 25 MW is unfinanceable under the 2045 rule.
