```
The plant will be located at the premises of Baxter Wilson power station.

The VAPS is proposing to have two emergency diesel-fired Internal Combustion (IC) engines. The engines will be limited to 100 hr/yr of operation or less in non-emergency operating modes due to the engines’ emergency classification status. The engines will not be constrained by an operating limit for legitimate emergency operating modes. One emergency diesel-fired IC engine is a 2,180-kilowatt (kW) emergency standby generator. The
proposed engine is a Mitsubishi Model S16R-Y2PTAW2-1 with a 2,932 brake horsepower (BHP) rating. The engine will only fire Ultra Low Sulfur Diesel (ULSD) with a maximum sulfur content of 15 parts per million weight (ppmw). The engine will be a USEPA-certified Tier 2 engine.
```

## State context

- Four utilities and three grid operators. Entergy Mississippi and Cooperative Energy sit in the Midcontinent Independent System Operator, called MISO, in its southern region (local resource zone 10). Mississippi Power is a Southern Company utility in the Southern balancing area, with its own interconnection queue on the Southern Company transmission site. The Tennessee Valley Authority, called TVA, serves the north of the state and runs its own queue on its OASIS site. Clarksdale Public Utilities owns Crossroads.
- The utility regulator is the Mississippi Public Service Commission. Docket numbers look like 2022-UA-153. Portal document links need a session, so cite the docket number and the document name, or the monthly docket list PDF for a docket's title and filing date. `harvest_psc_mississippi.py` reads the portal ahead of a sweep; copies stay in `work/psc_ms/`. TVA projects need no state certificate; TVA's environmental reviews and records of decision are the approval documents.
- Air permits come from the Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality, called MDEQ, through its Permit Board. Permits and activity are in enSearch Online at opcgis.deq.state.ms.us. The Permit Board posts monthly agendas and minutes; the 2021 minutes are missing from the minutes index. `harvest_permits_mississippi.py` reads all three and writes `work/permits/us-ms/<plant>.md`, plus `_unmatched.md` for power-like facilities GEM lacks.
- Entergy Mississippi's new combined cycle plants are Delta Blues (Greenville), Vicksburg (at the Baxter Wilson site) and Traceview (Madison County, called Madison County Advanced Power Station in its air permit). Each is one Mitsubishi M501JAC block in GEM.
- TVA's New Caledonia plant (Lowndes County) is six GE 7E.03 heavy-duty frames, so six units is right. A groundbreaking was held on October 22, 2025.
- Colossus 2 (Southaven area) is xAI's data center power plant. GEM holds the operating temporary turbines as unit 1 (495 MW) and the planned Solar Titan 350 set as unit 2 (1,240.5 MW). The MDEQ facility is MZX Tech LLC (agency interest 12592); GEM's owner name is MZT Tech LLC, so check the spelling.
- Plant Ratcliffe is the gas combined cycle of Mississippi Power's Kemper County project. The coal gasifier was cancelled. The GEM plant named Kemper County power station is TVA's separate four-turbine peaking plant.
- Jack Watson units 4 and 5 burned coal until 2015 and burn gas now; GEM holds the gas phase as timepoint 2 units. Morrow's coal units retired in 2018 and the gas combined cycle MOR1 replaced them.
