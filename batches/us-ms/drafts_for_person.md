## United States research tab, Mississippi row (row 35)

Regulator: Mississippi Public Service Commission (dockets like 2026-UA-5; the monthly docket list PDFs are citable). Air permits: Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality Permit Board agendas and minutes, and enSearch Online. TVA plants need no state certificate; TVA's environmental reviews are the approval documents.
