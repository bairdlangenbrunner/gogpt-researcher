# New Mexico Public Regulation Commission cases on generation (2026-11-19)

Cases filed 2021 or later whose captions are about generation, a resource plan, an abandonment, a data center or large load, or a power purchase agreement, plus the hand-checked list. A document has no address of its own in PRCe360: cite the case page link with the document number and title. Copies of documents read are in scopes/us-nm/work/psc/ and are not committed.
