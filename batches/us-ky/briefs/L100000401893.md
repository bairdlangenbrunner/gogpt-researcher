- Kentucky context: the state is split among four grids. East Kentucky Power Cooperative, Kentucky Power (an AEP company) and Duke Energy Kentucky are in PJM; Big Rivers Electric is in MISO and Owensboro Municipal Utilities joins MISO in 2027; LG&E and KU (PPL companies) run their own balancing authority outside any market; TVA's Marshall, Paradise and Shawnee plants are federal and outside any market and outside Kentucky PSC jurisdiction. ## State context

- **Grid operators.** PJM (planning queues XML; Kentucky rows carry state KY and a point of interconnection), MISO (getprojects JSON; the Big Rivers rows), LG&E and KU (an OASIS queue behind an OATI sign-in; projects are answered from commission cases and permits), TVA (an OASIS queue behind a sign-in; projects are answered from TVA's environmental reviews and board record).
